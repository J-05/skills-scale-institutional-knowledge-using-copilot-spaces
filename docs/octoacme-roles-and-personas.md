# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide organizational backing for a project and ensure that its outcomes remain aligned with strategic priorities. They remove issues that exceed the project team's authority and make or sponsor high-impact decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the business case, objectives, and significant scope changes
- Secure organizational support, funding, and resources
- Resolve escalated organizational impediments and competing priorities
- Review project health, risks, and outcomes at appropriate governance points

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receive status, risk, and escalation updates from the Project Manager
- Align strategic outcomes and trade-offs with Product Managers
- Support Developers and other delivery contributors by resolving issues outside the team's authority

---

## Business or Product Owners

### Role Summary
Business or Product Owners represent the outcomes a project must achieve and make day-to-day value and priority decisions. On some projects, this persona is fulfilled by the Product Manager; where the roles are separate, the Product Owner focuses on delivery-level decisions and acceptance.

### Responsibilities
- Translate business objectives into prioritized outcomes and acceptance criteria
- Make timely scope, priority, and trade-off decisions within delegated authority
- Confirm that delivered work meets the agreed needs and success measures
- Coordinate business feedback and communicate changes in desired outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align strategic direction and roadmap decisions with Product Managers and Executive Sponsors
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on priorities, scope changes, and delivery trade-offs
- Clarify requirements for Developers and Quality Leads and participate in acceptance activities

---

## Project Management Office (PMO) or Process Stewards

### Role Summary
The PMO or Process Steward maintains shared project management practices and helps teams apply them consistently. This role provides governance, reusable guidance, and cross-project learning without replacing the Project Manager's delivery accountability.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project templates, standards, lifecycle guidance, and reporting expectations
- Define and monitor useful delivery metrics and governance checkpoints
- Support onboarding and advise teams on applying the project management process
- Capture lessons learned and coordinate process improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coach Project Managers and provide visibility into cross-project risks or trends
- Help Executive Sponsors interpret governance information and make consistent decisions
- Use feedback from Product Managers, Developers, and retrospectives to improve shared practices

---

## Technical Leads or Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction and ensure implementation choices support the project's functional and quality goals. They make or facilitate technical decisions and surface technical dependencies and risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate the technical approach and significant architectural decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks and propose mitigations
- Guide design and implementation practices across Developers
- Evaluate feasibility, maintainability, security, performance, and other quality attributes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide the Project Manager with technical estimates, dependencies, risks, and decision impacts
- Partner with Product Managers to explain feasibility and delivery trade-offs
- Guide Developers through design reviews while preserving their responsibility for implementation and tests

---

## Quality or Testing Leads

### Role Summary
Quality or Testing Leads coordinate the practices and evidence needed to show that a project meets its acceptance and quality expectations. They make quality risks visible rather than treating testing as a final handoff.

### Responsibilities
- Define the validation strategy and quality criteria for the project
- Coordinate test planning, defect tracking, and quality-risk assessment
- Confirm that release-readiness evidence is complete and understandable
- Promote testability, automation, and continuous quality improvement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers on testability, coverage, and defect resolution
- Report quality status, trends, and release risks to the Project Manager
- Partner with Product Managers to validate acceptance criteria and outcome expectations

---

## Operations or Service Owners

### Role Summary
Operations or Service Owners represent the people and practices responsible for running, supporting, securing, and improving the delivered service. They bring operational readiness into planning instead of waiting until release.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational, reliability, security, support, and monitoring requirements
- Review deployment, rollback, incident-response, and support readiness
- Identify operational risks and dependencies and coordinate mitigation plans
- Accept or escalate service-readiness decisions within their authority

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers on non-functional requirements and deployability
- Provide the Project Manager with readiness status, operational risks, and transition plans
- Partner with Product Managers to balance customer value with supportability and service outcomes

---

## End Users or Customer Representatives

### Role Summary
End Users or Customer Representatives provide direct user context and feedback so that delivery decisions reflect real needs and outcomes. They participate in discovery, validation, demonstrations, and reviews as appropriate.

### Responsibilities
- Describe user needs, workflows, constraints, and measures of success
- Review proposed solutions and provide timely usability and outcome feedback
- Validate delivered behavior against user expectations and acceptance criteria
- Surface adoption concerns and opportunities for improvement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to shape problems, priorities, and acceptance criteria
- Follow the Project Manager's communication and feedback cadence
- Participate in demonstrations and validation activities with Developers and Quality Leads

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- A single person may fulfill multiple personas on a small project, but the responsibilities and decision boundaries remain distinct.
- Project teams should identify who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed for major activities so that handoffs and escalation paths are clear.
